# Owner sign-off and an independent AI check approve interpretations

Owner decision, 2026-11-21: replace ADR-0022's two-human review rule with
one human sign-off by the owner plus an independent AI reviewer as a second,
non-human check. Agents draft each interpretation with citations; the owner
signs it. Record the draft author, owner sign-off and independent AI review.

This supersedes the two-human rule wherever earlier ADRs refer to it,
including ADR-0024, ADR-0025, ADR-0027 and ADR-0031. Source citations,
source-hash freshness, written analysis and testing obligations remain.
A source change still requires re-review under the revised rule.
